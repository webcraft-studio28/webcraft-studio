The Process

What happens after you choose a package

A clear, predictable path from first call to launch — no surprises along the way.

01

Discovery Call

A 15-30 minute call, or a written questionnaire if that's easier, to understand your goals, content, and timeline.

02

Proposal & Scope

Fixed price and timeline confirmed in writing before any design work begins — no surprise invoices later.

03

Design & Build

First draft delivered in about 1-2 weeks for a single page, 2-4 weeks for a multi-page site.

04

Revisions

Two rounds of revisions are included in every package; additional rounds are billed separately.

05

Launch & Handoff

Site goes live, you get a walkthrough on updating it yourself, plus an optional care plan for ongoing support.

What's not included by default

Stock photography and copywriting (available as an add-on), hosting and domain costs (billed directly to you by the provider), and third-party app or plugin licensing fees.
