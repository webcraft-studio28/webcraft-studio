Why Ember Room

Built around the fire

Everything Over Fire

No gas ranges in this kitchen. Every dish touches the wood-fired hearth at some point, start to finish.

Seasonal, Local

The menu changes with what's actually in season. If a farm has a great week, so does the menu.

No Rush

One seating a night. Tables are yours for the evening, not turned over between courses.

The Experience

From the hearth to your table

Cooked Over Fire

Every dish is finished by hand at the hearth, stirred and watched, not plated from a heat lamp.

Brought to the Table

Servers bring each course out as it's ready, straight from the hearth, not staged under a warmer.

Shared, If You Like

Ask for the cart. Several mains and sides can come out family-style, table to table.
