Design Notes

Why this concept looks the way it does

01

The problem

Fitness apps compete in a saturated market where a landing page has to earn trust within seconds. Most fall back to stock photography and vague "get fit fast" claims that don't hold up with a data-driven audience.

02

The approach

A minimal, sticky, nav-less header and a single-column stacked hero built around one dominant phone mockup, instead of the side-by-side text-and-visual split used elsewhere. Plain-language copy over jargon, one uncluttered path toward a single call to action.

03

The result

A page structured around one goal (the download) with every section building toward that action, and a layout distinct enough from the studio's other work to show the same team can execute completely different registers.
