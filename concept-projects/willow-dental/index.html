Design Notes

Why this concept looks the way it does

The problem

Most local dental sites are either a generic template from practice-management software, or intimidating close-up stock photos of dental tools. Neither builds trust with someone who's already anxious about the visit.

The approach

A calm, soft-toned page with its own layout, not a reskinned template: a slim tab-style header with reviews built in, a single-column centered hero, and an alternating zigzag story instead of a card grid, each reason to choose Willow given its own full-width moment rather than competing for space in a box.

The result

A page that feels closer to a boutique wellness brand than a dental office, while still surfacing exactly what a new patient needs: services, pricing, hours, and a way to book.
