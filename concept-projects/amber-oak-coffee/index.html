Started in a garage. Still roasted by hand.

Amber & Oak started as a hobby roaster in a two-car garage, one 1kg batch at a time. These days the roaster's bigger, but the process hasn't changed: we still cup every batch before it goes out, and we still only roast what we can sell fresh that week.

Design Notes

Why this concept looks the way it does

The problem

Small-batch roasteries rarely have more than a social media page, which makes it hard for new customers to find hours, see prices, or understand what sets a hobby-turned-business apart from a chain.

The approach

A warm, editorial single page built around the roastery's own story instead of a generic template, with a custom cup illustration in place of stock photography and every piece of practical information a visitor needs reachable in one scroll.

The result

A fast, hand-coded page with zero page-builder bloat, and a visual identity distinct enough that it doesn't read as a template with a different logo dropped in.
