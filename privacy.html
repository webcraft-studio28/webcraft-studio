Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information this website collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It applies to webcraftstudio28@gmail.com and this site's pages, operated by Webcraft Studio.

What's collected

The only personal information this site actively collects is what you choose to submit through the contact form: your name, email address, project budget selection, and message. There are no user accounts, no e-commerce checkout, and no data collected through browsing alone beyond what's described below.

How the contact form works

Form submissions are processed by Formspree, a third-party form backend, and forwarded to Webcraft Studio's email inbox. Formspree acts as a data processor for this purpose; see Formspree's own privacy policy for how they handle data in transit. Your message is not used for anything beyond responding to your inquiry, and is not sold, rented, or shared with any other third party.

Local storage

This site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) for two small, non-identifying technical purposes: remembering your selected display currency on the Services page, and caching public exchange-rate data for up to 24 hours so it doesn't need to be re-fetched on every visit. This information stays on your device and is never sent to Webcraft Studio.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand overall traffic patterns, such as which pages are visited and roughly how visitors found the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data such as your approximate location, device/browser type, and on-site behavior; it does not receive your name, email, or anything you type into the contact form. See Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles this data. No advertising trackers or ad-retargeting scripts are used on this site.

Your rights

You can ask what information is held about you, request it be corrected, or request it be deleted, by emailing webcraftstudio28@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the "last updated" date at the top of this page will change too.

Questions

Reach out any time at webcraftstudio28@gmail.com.
